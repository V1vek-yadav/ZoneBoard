How the board and chart are calculated
Strength
(Price − yesterday's close) ÷ the stock's 14-day ATR. +1.5× means the stock has moved one and a half normal days' worth. This is why a 2% move in a quiet stock can outrank a 4% move in a volatile one.
OI buildup
Total futures OI across all expiries against yesterday. Price ↑ OI ↑ long buildup · price ↓ OI ↑ short buildup · price ↑ OI ↓ short covering · price ↓ OI ↓ long unwinding.
Liquidity score (0–100)
0 when choppy: a move under 0.5× ATR, or price on the wrong side of VWAP. Otherwise up to 40 for strength, 25 when OI confirms the move (10 for covering/unwinding), 20 for where price sits in the day's range, and 15 for volume against its usual pace.
Order block
The last opposite-colour candle before a move that closes beyond the latest swing high/low and covers at least 2× the 5-minute ATR. Fresh = untouched, tested = price came back into it. Zones disappear once a candle closes through them.
Liquidity levels
Where stop orders cluster: previous-day high/low (PDH/PDL), the first 15 minutes' range (ORH/ORL), and equal highs/lows (EQH/EQL), meaning two swing points within 0.3× ATR. Swept = traded through and closed back; taken = closed beyond.
Data
Live version: Kotak Neo Trade API, read-only (quotes, candles, instrument list). Board refreshes every minute from 9:15 to 15:30 IST; outside market hours it shows the last session.

These are first-version rules, not proven edges. The live version saves every minute's data so the rules can be tested before anyone trades on them.
